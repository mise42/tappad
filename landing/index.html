Use scenarios

Four ways your phone becomes useful at the desk.

Control from away

Use your phone as a touchpad when you do not want to reach for the mouse and keyboard, or when you are sitting away from the screen.

02

Input across screens

Type, dictate, or paste on the phone with your favorite input method, then send the text into the focused desktop app.

03

Trigger desktop actions

Run common desktop actions from the phone: screenshots, media controls, app launch, window control, recording, and more.

04

Skip extra hardware

Keep working when you did not bring a mouse, the trackpad is out of reach, or you do not want to buy another input device.
